How does cash on delivery work?
You place the order on WhatsApp, we confirm it, and the courier delivers the watch to your address. You pay the courier in cash. No advance payment is needed.
How long does delivery take?
Most orders reach major cities in 2 to 4 working days. Remote areas can take a little longer. Delivery is free everywhere in Pakistan.
What if the watch has a problem?
Message us within 7 days of delivery with a photo or short video. If the piece is faulty or not what you ordered, we replace it.
How do I choose the right size?
Open the size guide, enter your wrist measurement and we show the best case size from 38 mm to 44 mm, with a live preview on a wrist.
Is the price shown on the website?
Pricing is shared privately on WhatsApp so we can confirm the current rate and availability for the exact piece you're interested in.
